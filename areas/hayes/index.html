Before you book a skip

Why a skip is the harder option

A skip

  • Needs a permit from Hillingdon if it goes on the road
  • Sits outside for days
  • You do all the loading
  • Neighbours fill it overnight
  • You pay for the space whether you fill it or not

Us

  • No permit. Nothing is left on the road
  • In and out, usually within the hour
  • We do the lifting, from anywhere on the property
  • Nothing left outside to fill
  • You pay for the space you actually use

Questions

The usual questions

Do I need a permit?

No. Permits are a skip problem — a skip left on a public road needs a licence from the council. Nothing of ours stays on the road, so there's nothing to license.

How soon can you come?

Often the same day. Call early and you'll usually get a slot that afternoon.

Do I have to carry it out to the road?

No. We carry it out — upstairs flats, back gardens, lofts and garages included.

What does it cost?

It depends on volume and what the waste is, so we price from a photo. Send a picture of the pile and your postcode and a fixed price comes back, usually within the hour.

What happens to the waste?

It goes to a licensed transfer station and gets sorted, and as much as possible is recycled. You get a waste transfer note by email.
