Before you book a skip

Skip or us?

A skip

  • Needs a council permit if it goes on the road, and a pavement licence in much of central London
  • Sits outside your house for days
  • You do all the loading yourself
  • Neighbours fill it overnight
  • Fixed size — you pay for the space whether you fill it or not

Us

  • No permit. Nothing is left on the road
  • In and out, usually within the hour
  • We do the lifting, from anywhere on the property
  • Nothing left outside to fill
  • You pay for the space you actually use

Why it matters who you call

Your rubbish is still your responsibility

Under your duty of care as a waste producer, if the man with a van you hired dumps your waste in a lane, the fine can come back to you — the letters in the pile lead to your door, not his.

Paper trail

Every job comes with a waste transfer note listing what we took and where it went.

Licensed disposal

Everything goes to a licensed transfer station where it's sorted, and as much as possible is recycled rather than landfilled.

Questions

The usual questions

Do I need to be there?

Not always. If you can tell us where the waste is and how to get in, plenty of jobs are done while the customer is at work. We send photos when it's finished.

Do I need a permit?

No. Permits are a skip problem. Nothing of ours stays on the road.

How soon can you come?

Often the same day. Call early and you'll usually get a slot that afternoon.

Do I have to move everything outside first?

No. We carry it out — upstairs flats, back gardens, lofts and garages included.

How do I pay?

On the day, once the job's done and you're happy.

What happens to my stuff?

It goes to a licensed transfer station and gets sorted. Anything reusable is kept out of landfill where we can.
