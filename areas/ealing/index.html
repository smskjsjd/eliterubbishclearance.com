What we clear in Ealing

The jobs that come up here

Controlled parking zones

A skip in a CPZ bay needs the bay suspended as well as the permit. Two fees, two waits. We don't leave anything on the street, so there's nothing to license.

Mansion blocks and converted flats

Lots of Ealing's flats are conversions with a shared stair and no lift. We carry down. It's the job, not an extra.

Victorian and Edwardian terraces

High ceilings, deep gardens, and usually a long carry from the back to the road. Priced on what's there, not on how far we have to walk.

We can't take asbestos, chemicals, fuels, oils, gas cylinders or clinical waste. Ask us and we'll tell you who can.

Questions

The usual questions

Do I need a permit?

No. Permits are a skip problem — a skip left on a public road needs a licence from the council. Nothing of ours stays on the road, so there's nothing to license.

How soon can you come?

Often the same day. Call early and you'll usually get a slot that afternoon.

Do I have to carry it out to the road?

No. We carry it out — upstairs flats, back gardens, lofts and garages included.

What does it cost?

It depends on volume and what the waste is, so we price from a photo. Send a picture of the pile and your postcode and a fixed price comes back, usually within the hour.

What happens to the waste?

It goes to a licensed transfer station and gets sorted, and as much as possible is recycled. You get a waste transfer note by email.
