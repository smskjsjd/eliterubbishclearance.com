What it's for

The jobs the big grabs turn down

We don't chase 15 tonnes of spoil off an open building site — there are 8-wheelers for that. We're for the awkward ones: terraced streets, back gardens, gated estates, controlled parking, and loads too small for anyone else to bother with.

Garden clearances

Soil, turf, overgrowth, old sheds, decking, fence panels and patio slabs. The crane lifts it over the fence, so nothing comes through your house.

Rubble and renovation waste

Broken concrete, hardcore, bricks, timber and strip-out debris from extensions and refits.

Small muck away

Spoil from footings, extensions and garden levelling, where a full-size grab can't get near the pile.

Bulk household clearance

Where there's more than a van's worth but less than a full skip lorry's.

Questions

The usual questions

Do I need a permit for a grab lorry?

No. Permits apply to skips left on a public road. A grab loads and drives away, so there's nothing to license. If the lorry has to stand on a red route or in a controlled bay we'll tell you before booking.

Will it fit down my road?

That's the reason we run this lorry rather than an 8-wheeler. Send a photo of the access and we'll give you a straight answer — including if the answer is no.

Do you take half loads?

Yes. Big grab firms want a full load before they'll make the trip. We don't.

What happens to the waste?

It goes to a licensed transfer station, gets sorted, and as much as possible is recycled rather than landfilled. You get a waste transfer note.

Are you licensed?
